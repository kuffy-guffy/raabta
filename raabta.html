Welcome to RGIPT

Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT), in Jais, Amethi (formerly in Raebareli), Uttar Pradesh, India, is a research and education institute focusing on STEM and petroleum industry. It is an institute of national importance equivalent to IITs. It was formally opened in July 2008.

It has been accorded the Institute of National Importance status and a governance structure similar to that available to IITs It admits undergraduate students from the rank list of students who have qualified for Joint Entrance Examination – Advanced (JEE Advanced) Examination.

More about Rgipt

Started academic sessions in June 2008 at a temporary campus at Rae Bareli. On 15 October 2016, the institute moved from temporary campus to the permanent campus at Jais.

This campus offers the BTech, MTech, MBA, PhD programs and other research related activities. The cost for setting up this campus was ₹8.61 billion (US$89 million).

From 2020 onwards, The Institute began offering BTech Courses in Computer Science Engineering and Electronics Engineering.

In the year 2021, a new four year B.Tech. programme in Mathematics & Computing has been started at RGIPT with the goal of setting the stage for developing skills in mathematics and computer science.

Energia

Energia is an annual festival of three days of activities including sporting events, talent playoffs and similar activities. It was separated from the annual cultural festival in 2017 and since then, sporting events are held in this festival. Sawai godara is the current Energy Chief and prime person behind sport activities in the college In 2019, this event marked its third anniversary as sports were part of annual cultural fest since the beginning of the institute. This festival is currently financed by the institute while some of the amount is fetched by sponsorship

Kaltarang

Kaltarang is the annual cultural and sports fest of Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology, Amethi. It is a themed fest, generally held in the month of February every year, since the very first time in 2011. It is a three-day fest having events like rock band performance, fashion show, rock band competition, stand-up comedy, cyber gaming, themed events, various sports events

Student Affairs Councils

Science and Technology Council

Responsibilities

  • Technical Clubs: Oversees specialized student groups including the Kode Club (programming), Robotics Club, E-Cell (Entrepreneurship Cell), and astronomy/aeromodelling cells.
  • Annual Festival: Organizes Urjotsav, the institute's national-level annual techno-cultural festival that hosts coding hackathons, robotics combats, and guest lectures.
  • Skill Development: Conducts regular workshops, hands-on bootcamps, and technical competitions for students across branches like Petroleum, Chemical, and Computer Science Engineering.

Social Services Council

Core Initiatives

  • Gyanarpan: A flagship evening education program where RGIPT students provide free academic tutoring and mentorship to underprivileged school children from local communities.
  • ARPAN: A comprehensive community welfare program focusing on health camps, clean drive initiatives, and social awareness campaigns.
  • Blood Donation Drives: Periodic campus-wide camps organized in collaboration with local government hospitals and blood banks.
  • Food Donations: Collection and distribution drives conducted for underserved families, especially ahead of the winter season and major festivals.

Cultural Council:

Core Activities & Festivals

  • Kaltarang: The institute's signature national-level annual cultural festival, featuring competitive events in music, dance, fashion, drama, and literature.
  • Festival Celebrations: Organizes on-campus celebrations for major cultural events and festivals, including Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, and Diwali.
  • Specialized Clubs: Coordinates diverse performance and creative groups, including vocal/instrumental music, street play (Nukkad Natak), choreography, and literary arts.

Sports Council

Core Activities & Events

  • Kridotsav: The premier annual sports festival of RGIPT, bringing together students to compete in track, field, and indoor sports.
  • Inter-Hostel Matches: Regular internal leagues and tournaments, including cricket, football, volleyball, and badminton matches.
  • Facility Management: Maintains the campus sports complexes, including the gymnasium, basketball courts, badminton courts, and the main sports grounds.
